\src{A}{S}To see how SMOTE is used outside the method literature, we audited the applications that the screen of Section~\ref{sec:search} excluded under E1: <<excluded_E1>> records that apply existing resampling to a problem of their own. We drew <<use_drawn>> of them at random, 100 from each of three periods (up to 2020, 2021 to 2023, and since 2024, which hold <<use_U1_frame>>, <<use_U2_frame>>, and <<use_U3_frame>> records). \src{A}{S}AI sessions coded each from its title and abstract under a written codebook (online appendix, Section~S5), and the first 50 of each period in the order of the draw also from the full text, of which <<use_ft_obtained>> could be obtained from open copies or through the authors' university library. \src{A}{S}The <<use_rulings_recoded>> records that this coding took to propose a resampling method of their own were coded again under the rulings of Section~\ref{sec:search}, by which a standard generator, or a chain of existing steps that the paper does not compare with other resampling, is existing resampling. Of the <<use_drawn>> records, <<use_application>> are indeed applications. \src{A}{S}A second coding of all <<d42_use_n>> records by separate AI sessions, blind to the first and from the same texts, agreed with it on <<d42_use_ab_pct>>\% of the values coded for every record (Cohen's kappa <<d42_use_ab_kappa_lo>> to <<d42_use_ab_kappa_hi>> by field, lowest for the kind of validation) and on <<d42_use_ft_pct>>\% of the safeguards coded from the <<d42_use_ft_n>> full texts. An AI session settled the <<d42_use_items>> differences from the records, seeing the two codes in random order; <<d42_use_corrected>> of them changed a first code, and the counts of this section use the settled codes. <<?d42_chk_use_n>>\src{S}{}The authors checked <<d42_chk_use_n>> of the settled codes against the records: every change that can alter a count of this section, and random samples of the other changes, of the codes the adjudication kept, and of the codes both coders gave, and found all of them correct.<</d42_chk_use_n>>

\src{A}{S}Medicine is the largest domain: <<use_dom_medicine>> of the <<use_application>> applications (<<use_dom_medicine_pct>>\%; <<use_med_w>>\% with the periods weighted by their size, 95\% interval <<use_med_w_lo>> to <<use_med_w_hi>>) build a model for diagnosis, prognosis, or another clinical or public-health decision. Finance follows (<<use_dom_finance>>), then social and text data (<<use_dom_social>>), security (<<use_dom_security>>), and software engineering (<<use_dom_software>>). In <<use_ind>> applications (<<use_ind_pct>>\%), the model is meant to inform a decision about a person, such as a patient, a borrower, a student, or a customer. Plain SMOTE is the resampler in <<use_smote>> (<<use_smote_pct>>\%), <<use_tab>> work on tabular data, and <<use_deep>> train a deep network.

\src{A}{S}The full texts show how the safeguards of Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} fare in practice. \src{S}{}Of the <<use_ft_obtained>> full texts obtained, <<use_ft>> are applications, and the counts of this paragraph are of these <<use_ft>>. Among them, <<use_ft_stated>> state that the resampling was fitted on the training data only, <<use_ft_before_split>> resampled the whole data set before splitting it, which is the leak of Section~\ref{sec:leakage}, and <<use_ft_nr>> do not say; with the periods weighted by their size, an estimated <<use_ft_before_w>>\% of the applications resample before the split (95\% interval <<use_ft_before_w_lo>> to <<use_ft_before_w_hi>>). \src{A}{S}The leak shows no trend across the periods: it appears in <<use_U1_before>> of <<use_U1_ft>> full texts up to 2020, <<use_U2_before>> of <<use_U2_ft>> from 2021 to 2023, and <<use_U3_before>> of <<use_U3_ft>> since 2024, although more papers since 2024 state that they fitted the resampling on the training data (<<use_U3_stated>> of <<use_U3_ft>>, against <<use_U1_stated>> of <<use_U1_ft>> and <<use_U2_stated>> of <<use_U2_ft>> before). In <<use_ft_testbal>>, the reported results were computed on test data that had themselves been resampled, so the scores do not describe the natural class distribution; for this code and the previous one, arithmetic on the numbers a paper reports counts as evidence (ruling D of the authors). The same model trained without resampling is a comparator in <<use_ft_none>>, class weights or a tuned threshold in <<use_ft_weights|w>>, and calibration is assessed in <<use_ft_calib|w>>. Accuracy is reported in <<use_ft_acc>>, ROC-AUC in <<use_ft_roc>>, and PR-AUC in <<use_ft_pr|w>>. Data from another source validate the model in <<use_ft_external|w>>, a prospective evaluation appears in <<use_ft_prosp|w>>, and results from deployment in <<use_ft_deployed|w>>. \src{S}{}The other <<use_ft_missing>> papers drawn for full-text coding could not be obtained and may differ.
