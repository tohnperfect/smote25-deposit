\src{S}{}Section~\ref{sec:whichmethod} reports a rerun of our own, in which ten SMOTE-family methods and four baselines run under one protocol on the <<rr_tasks>> tasks of our reference collection (Appendices~\ref{app:reference} and~\ref{app:benchmark}). We added a tabular foundation model to this rerun: TabPFN-<<tp_model>>, the default model of version <<tp_package>> of the \texttt{tabpfn} package, with its default settings, without resampling and after SMOTE, on the same folds. \src{A}{S}It ran on the graphics processor of the Apple M5 Pro of the core tier and gave a result in every fold of <<tp_tasks>> of the <<rr_tasks>> tasks, in <<tp_families>> families. On musk and smoke detection it ran out of the processor's memory, and on ijcnn1 and one ecoli task SMOTE could not run in every fold; under the rule fixed before the results, every comparison uses the other <<tp_tasks>> tasks. SMOTE made the model worse on all three measures: over families, it lowered ROC-AUC by a median of <<tp_sm_roc>> and PR-AUC by <<tp_sm_pr>>, and raised the Brier score by <<tp_sm_brier>> (Holm-adjusted Wilcoxon $p < <<tp_sm_holm_below>>$; Bayesian probabilities that the model is better without it <<tp_sm_bay_roc>>, <<tp_sm_bay_pr>> and <<tp_sm_bay_brier>>). Without resampling, the model ranked better than the random forest and gradient boosting of the rerun, with or without SMOTE, by medians of <<tp_vs_roc_min>> to <<tp_vs_roc_max>> in ROC-AUC and <<tp_vs_pr_min>> to <<tp_vs_pr_max>> in PR-AUC (Holm-adjusted $p < <<tp_vs_holm_below>>$; Bayesian probability at least <<tp_vs_bay_min>> that the model is better). Its Brier score was lower than that of gradient boosting, with or without SMOTE, and of the random forest after SMOTE, by <<tp_vs_brier_min>> to <<tp_vs_brier_max>>, and on par with that of the random forest without resampling (online appendix, Section~S7). Each learner ran with one configuration, and the cells of the random forest and gradient boosting are those of the rerun, <<tp_rf_cloud>> of the <<tp_tasks>> tasks from the cloud workspace, so these comparisons mix two machines on the same folds.
