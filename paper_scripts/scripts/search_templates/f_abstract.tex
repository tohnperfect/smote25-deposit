\src{S}{}\textbf{Methods.} We audited a curated corpus of 76 papers, 37 of which propose a SMOTE-family generator, and tested its coverage with a systematic search of <<screened>> records in the PRISMA 2020 format. From the corpus we collected 1,756 dataset entries, 1,511 published comparisons with SMOTE and the evaluation design of 71 studies; from the search we drew <<use_drawn>> applications of SMOTE at random.
