\src{A}{S}The records were screened on title and abstract under these criteria by an AI assistant (Claude Opus 5.5), in separate sessions of up to 200 records that followed a written codebook (online appendix, Section~S5), in three looks. A first pass decided every record and recorded a code, a confidence, and a one-sentence reason. A second look, blind to the first, screened again the <<second_n>> records that the first pass had marked unsure or decided with low or medium confidence; on these hard cases, the two looks agree on <<second_agreement_pct>>\% (Cohen's $\kappa = <<second_kappa>>$). A third look decided the <<decided_third>> records on which the two disagreed or either was unsure, weighing both earlier reasons, and marked <<third_unsure>> of them unsure. Two checks bear on the first pass. \src{S}{}On <<pilot_n>> records drawn at random before the screen and screened in a pilot by other sessions, the two runs agree on <<pilot_agreement_pct>>\% ($\kappa = <<pilot_kappa>>$), and all <<pilot_differences>> differences involve unsure; and of <<known_n>> SMOTE-family papers of the corpus that were matched to the records at that stage, it included <<known_include>> and marked <<known_unsure>> unsure.

\src{S}{}Another AI model (Claude Fable 5.2) then checked the screen, in separate sessions, under instructions that its own sessions wrote from the authors' criteria (archived with the data); Figure~\ref{fig:workflow} shows where this check, and every other step, sits and who did it. \src{A}{S}Blind to the screen's decisions, the check decided a random sample of <<check_blind_inc_n>> included and <<check_blind_exc_n>> excluded records; of the <<check_blind_decided>> that it included or excluded, it agrees with the screen on <<check_blind_agree>> (<<check_blind_pct>>\%, Cohen's $\kappa = <<check_blind_kappa>>$). It then decided, from title and abstract and under the rulings set out below, the <<check_unsure_all>> records that the screen had left unsure (<<check_unsure_include_all>> include, <<check_unsure_exclude_all>> exclude, <<check_unsure_unsure_all>> unsure), corrected <<check_sample_corrections>> decisions in its sample of included records, and checked the codes that AI sessions had given to <<check_codes_rows>> records of the two samples described below, correcting <<check_codes_corrected>>. Its decisions and corrections replace those of the screen.

\src{A}{S}Some records were deposits that their repository has since removed: of the <<zenodo_checked>> records with a Zenodo DOI, Zenodo had removed <<excluded_withdrawn>>, nearly all as spam. A record whose only version has been removed cannot be read in full, so the authors excluded all <<excluded_withdrawn>> under a code of their own (E-WITHDRAWN, ruling A).

\src{A}{S}The check also raised questions about where criterion I1 ends, which the authors settled with three further rulings (an addendum to the codebook records them; online appendix, Section~S5). A chain of existing resampling steps counts as the paper's own method only if the paper presents it as its contribution and compares it with at least one other way of resampling (ruling B). A standard generator used without a stated change (a plain GAN, conditional GAN, WGAN-GP, CTGAN, VAE, diffusion or language model, KDE or GMM sampling, a copula, or standard data augmentation) never counts as the paper's own, even when it is chained with other steps and compared (B2). The task does not decide scope: segmentation, regression recast as classes, survival analysis, object detection, continual and zero-shot learning, and ranking count like classification (C). The check applied B and B2 to the records it decided. For the other included records, AI sessions (Claude Opus 5.5) recorded from each title and abstract the facts that the rulings turn on, with a confidence, and a script applied the rulings to these facts. Where this pass and the check read the same <<ruleb_sample_includes_both>> included records, the pass was the stricter of the two: it fails <<ruleb_sample_pass_excluded>> of them under B or B2, among them all <<ruleb_sample_check_excluded>> that the check excluded. \src{A}{S}Of the other <<ruleb_applied>> included records, <<ruleb_failing_applied>> fail B or B2: the <<ruleb_excluded>> whose facts were recorded with high confidence are excluded under E1, and the remaining <<ruleb_unsure>> are marked unsure, together with the <<unsure_check>> that the check left unsure. \src{A}{S}The authors applied ruling C to the check's exclusions whose notes cite the task: they restored the four that oversample a rare class within it and kept out, under I1, the four that make data for unseen or for all classes.
