Codes I was least sure of:
- R04275 inside_folds=before-split: SMOTE-ENN was applied after the 70:30 split, but to the training and test sets separately (Table 5). Coded before-split because the test metrics were computed on the resampled test set (recall 0.899=178/198, accuracy 0.926=339/366) and the tuning CV apparently ran on resampled training data; not a resample-then-split case.
- R15526 inside_folds=stated / test_balanced=no: rests on a single results sentence ("Balancing the training data with ADASYN"); the methods do not say when ADASYN was applied. Could reasonably be nr/nr.
- R22074 validation=external: the authors call the 54-pump 2022-2025 evaluation "post-deployment", but it reads as a retrospective batch run; deployed or prospective are arguable.
- R04991 validation=internal: the authors call the 42-patient test set external, but it comes from the same TCGA-LGG cohort.
- R01271 validation=nr, inside_folds=nr: unsupervised clustering with no split; evaluated on the same images' original pixels. is_application=yes, although the paper is close to a comparison of resampling methods.
- R05027 resampler=random-oversampling: the source (a meeting abstract) says only "oversampling of TxE samples"; inside_folds and test_balanced are nr because of the "equal proportion ... across all datasets" sentence.
- Modality tabular for features extracted from images (R04275 UAV features, R04991 radiomics); image or mixed are arguable.
