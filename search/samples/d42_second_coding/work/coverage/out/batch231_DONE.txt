Codes I was least sure of:
- R00655 (ICOTE): label Not counted, generator other. The synthetic points are aiNet memory antibodies: random antibodies are cloned, mutated by Ab + a(Ab - Ag) and suppressed. The update runs along a line between an antibody and an antigen, but the antibody is not a data instance, so I did not count it as seed-partner interpolation. A checker could read the "immune centroids" as convex combinations; I do not. inside_folds nr: the method "extends a training set" and the paper uses the KEEL 5-fold partitions, but it never states resampling inside the folds. Its AUC is (1+TPR-FPR)/2; I coded it ROC-AUC.
- R00042 (de la Calleja & Fuentes 2007, 2-page paper): Core, because the partner is the mean of the 5 nearest positive neighbours, a convex combination. Both N (weighted distance, averaged neighbours) and P (combination of more than two instances) are yes, which may count one change twice. The method "adds the synthetic instance to the original data set" but 10-fold CV is not linked to it, so inside_folds nr. The experiments only compare oversampling amounts of 100-1000%, all reported with none selected, so tuning default, vs_smote no, vs_none no. The method is the paper's own, so ruling B does not apply.
- R03007 (DP-SMOTE): the operators of Eq. (5) were lost in text extraction. P = no rests on the prose "linear interpolation between cluster cores and other instances", with a uniform in (0,1). inside_folds nr: Sec. 4.2 oversamples each dataset and then classifies, but Algorithm 2 takes "the training set" as input. tuning default: dc = 2% is the standard value set beforehand, although the later dc analysis on the same CV results also favours 2%.
- R10647 (NR-Modified SMOTE): the KNN noise removal before generation is coded S (minority points barred from seeding), not F. Ruling B passed: the parts are known, but the paper presents the method as its contribution and compares it with SMOTE, ADASYN and NR-SMOTE. measures include ROC-AUC from the AUC column of Table X. inside_folds before-split: the data were balanced and then classified with 10-fold CV, and the confusion matrices sum to the balanced totals.
