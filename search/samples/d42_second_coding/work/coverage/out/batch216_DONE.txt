Codes I was least sure of:
- R00044 (BootOS): Core with P and L. New point = mean of seed and its k nearest minority neighbours (k=1 in the experiments, so the midpoint), run at every active-learning iteration. N left at no (k fixed at 1, "similarity functions" not specified). n_datasets=38 counts each OntoNotes noun as one dataset (could be read as 1 corpus). Recall is macro-averaged over senses (equals balanced accuracy), coded as recall as named.
- R01023 (FID): Not counted, generator other rather than distribution. Each feature of a new minority row is a fuzzy-weighted mean of observed values near evenly spaced centres over that feature's range, filled feature by feature, so no seed-partner interpolation and no fitted distribution sampled. Tuning coded default although ROS/SMOTE rates (200/500/700%) are not tied to the reported tables.
- R00865 (banknotes): Not counted. The paper drops SMOTE's interpolation for image flips/rotations plus a pixel-wise minimum (darker RGB) overlay of two minority images; read as "derives from SMOTE but drops its generator", not as placement in a region (Core/Partial with P). n_datasets=2 (two-grade and four-grade tasks; seven-grade not oversampled). Protocol holdout: fixed 100/70 split, runs repeated 10 times. measures include other for PR curves.
- R10073 (solar DDPM): ruling B2. The oversampler used for the imbalance experiments is a standard class-conditional DDPM with classifier-free guidance; the continuous X-ray and ceVAE-conditioned variants (possible "new conditioning") are only judged on image quality and never used to add minority data.
- R00168 (SMRBF): E1 without ruling B. SMOTE applied once, unchanged, before training; the paper calls SMRBF a classification methodology (memetic-trained RBF network, three-class growth model). Borderline with the I1 clause for SMOTE combined with a learner, since it is compared with OverSampling and SmoteOverSampling.
